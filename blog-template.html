Update in development

Hey sailors and survivors! We are working on a few features for Raftblock that are important before release. We want to be more transparent about the development so we are going to show you some behind the scenes stuff.

Abandoned Rafts

Abandoned rafts are a rare event that appears from time to time around the player. They serve as a source of extra resources. Abandoned rafts will sink when hit or collided with a projectile / punch and drop loot. There are three types.

Crates

There are many crates that can be obtained in various ways. These crates can be opened to get loot or they can be sold to the Wandering Sailor! Crates are a main part of the explorers' guild "Treasures n Trades".

Thank you for all the support! Raftblock is still in its early stages and we have a lot of things in the works.

There is currently no playable build. but we are slowly getting there.
